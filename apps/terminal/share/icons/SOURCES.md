The workspace navigation icons are upstream Phosphor `squares-four` regular and
fill assets from [Phosphor core](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core), revision
`2b75f3ad12b420c9504ef05df8d2564a28f8500e`. The SVG path geometry and
viewBox are unchanged. The upstream MIT license is in `PHOSPHOR-LICENSE`.

Workspace toolbar icons use the regular assets from the same upstream revision.
Only the display dimensions (24×24) and theme-aware fill (`currentColor`) are set;
path geometry and viewBox remain upstream-authored.
