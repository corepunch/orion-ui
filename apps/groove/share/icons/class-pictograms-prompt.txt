Asset: compact category pictograms overlaid on Groove sample cards.
Generate without a reference image so the old detailed rendering does not

Primary request: create a cohesive family of compact, square-like 3D instrument
icons. Preserve dimensional studio rendering and smooth color gradients, but
build each object from a few chunky, toy-like forms. Each icon should fit a
square footprint and read clearly at 32–64 px. Keep necks, handles, stands,
and other extensions very short and tucked in; angle long instruments within
their cells. Give every icon a similarly full, balanced visual weight.

Style: polished stylized 3D, softly rounded shapes, broad bevels, gentle
contact shading, and smooth restrained gradients. Use a simple soft key light
and clear color blocking. Keep materials matte to satin, with only a few broad
highlights. Avoid photorealistic materials and tiny reflections. Limit each
icon to a handful of broad parts; simplify strings, keys, buttons, rims, and
hardware into large marks or omit them. The set should feel dimensional and
friendly without looking intricate or noisy.

Composition: one complete atlas, exactly 6 columns by 3 rows of equal square
cells, 2:1 overall ratio. One centered icon per cell, fully contained with
clear transparent padding. Preserve true transparency; no cell dividers,
background plates, borders, or detached shadows.

Broad color cues: red drums and bass, blue guitar, warm wood percussion and
violin, brass cymbals and trumpet, ivory keys, charcoal organ and synth, red
lips, black vinyl, red-and-gold FX.

Avoid flat vector artwork, photorealism, intricate 3D rendering, tiny hardware,
thin stands, long narrow necks, many strings, screws, small knobs, repeated
tiny keys, surface textures, sharp specular glints, text, logos, extra or
missing objects, clipped silhouettes, and stray fragments. Output the complete

The sample-card painter overlays each icon at 75% of card height. Favor a
compact square silhouette and broad readable forms over instrument accuracy.

Packing: split the complete generated atlas into its 18 glyphs, remove detached
