Groove piano, bass guitar and guitar samples

Upstream: FluidR3 3.1, Frank Wen
Archive: https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/f/fluid-soundfont/fluid-soundfont_3.1.orig.tar.gz
Source: fluid-soundfont-3.1/FluidR3_GM.sf2
License: FLUID-LICENSE (upstream COPYING, MIT)

acoustic.sf2 contains only the seven presets listed in acoustic.json:
Yamaha Grand Piano, Bright Yamaha Grand, Fingered Bass, Steel String Guitar,
Clean Guitar, Palm Muted Guitar and Distortion Guitar.
No drum, synth, pad or choir preset is included. Wah uses Clean Guitar with a
filter in Groove. The remaining Groove instruments retain their original models.

The subset preserves the original PCM samples and key/velocity zones, envelopes,
loop points and tuning. acoustic.json records source/output SHA-256 values.
Rebuild from the upstream source:

python3 tools/groove_audio/subset_soundfont.py FluidR3_GM.sf2 apps/groove/share/instruments/acoustic.sf2 --preset 0:0 --preset 0:1 --preset 0:25 --preset 0:27 --preset 0:28 --preset 0:30 --preset 0:33 --manifest apps/groove/share/instruments/acoustic.json

Playback: TinySoundFont; source revision and MIT license are in
apps/groove/third_party/TSF-SOURCE.txt and TSF-LICENSE.

No eJay, MTV Music Generator or game recording is included in this bank.
