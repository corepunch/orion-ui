# Groove Support

Groove is a music arrangement app for iPad. Browse the sound library, drag
blocks onto the eight-track timeline, and press Play to hear your arrangement.
To try a complete song, choose a demo from the **File** menu.

If your version has **File → Record Sample**, use it to record a sound. If the
microphone is unavailable, open iPad Settings and check Groove's microphone
permission. After granting permission, return to Groove and press Record.

Use **File → Open Song** to find a project saved in Groove. If your version has
**Export Project** and **Import Song** in the File menu, you can use them to
share a copy of your project or open a project someone shared with you.

For help or privacy questions, email [Groove Support](mailto:igor.chernakov@gmail.com).
You can also [open a Groove support issue][issues] to report a bug. Include your
Groove version, iPad model, iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the
problem. Issues are public, so keep private information and recordings out of
the report.
