The desk

Every page answers one question the shift asks

Who arrives, who leaves, who still owes, which rooms are ready, can we close the day. The sidebar switches pages; the ribbon holds the commands.

Reservations

Book a guest without leaving the form

Choose a room and dates, pick a guest on file or type a new guest's name, and save with Ctrl+S. Every booking shows its total, what's paid and the balance.

  • Overlapping stays and rooms out of service are refused before saving
  • Find any booking by guest, room, phone or reservation number
  • Extend, shorten, cancel or mark a no-show from the ribbon
Walk-in guest

A guest at the desk without a booking, in one window

Name, nights and guests. Lodge lists the rooms free from tonight, ready rooms first. Check in now creates the guest, the stay, the check-in and the payment together, or nothing at all.

  • A room that isn't clean can't be checked into
  • A guest already on file is found again by name
Night audit

Close the day from a checklist

Every arrival handled, every departure checked out, bills settled, empty rooms clean, and today's money by payment method. Close today unlocks once nothing red is left; a closed day can't be changed.

Assistant · Ctrl+K

Ask in your own words

Type what you need the way you'd say it to a colleague. Enter shows what Lodge would do; Enter again does it. It runs inside Lodge, with no network, and follows the same rules as the buttons.

Why it looks like this

Built from what small hotels complain about

Before the redesign we read what owners and front-desk staff of small hotels say about their software. Each complaint became a decision.

What staff sayWhat Lodge does
Too many clicks and stacked windows; new staff need weeks to feel confident.Daily work starts from Today; check-in in two actions; walk-in in one window; shortcuts for every daily command.
No picture of the house, only search results.A two-week room calendar with real dates; double-click a room to book it.
Per-booking fees, add-ons and contracts that renew themselves.Free and open source. No account, no per-room or per-booking charge.
When the Internet drops, the desk stops.Everything runs locally. Nothing at the desk needs a network.
Data is hard to get out when you leave.The hotel owns its data file, and reservations, guests and payments export to CSV in one click.
Guests sent to rooms that weren't ready.Room status on Today and in the calendar; check-in refuses a room that isn't clean; a printed cleaning list.
The night audit is a chore done by hand.One checklist; the day closes when nothing red is left.
Support is slow to answer.Refusals say what to do next, and the assistant answers how-to requests on the spot.
Get Lodge

Build it, open Setup, add the sample hotel

Lodge is part of Orion UI. Build it from source on Linux, macOS or Windows. In Setup, choose the country and currency, then Add sample hotel to try every page with nine rooms and a few guests dated around today.

Pre-built downloads will be listed on the releases page.

# build
git clone --recursive https://github.com/corepunch/orion-ui
cd orion-ui
make share build/bin/lodge

# run, with the hotel's data in its own folder
build/bin/lodge --data ~/hotel --lang de
